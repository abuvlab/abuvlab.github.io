# Anders M. Buvarp

**Statistical Signal Processing · Robust AI · Wireless Communications · Quantum Information**

I am a researcher at the Norwegian University of
Science and Technology (NTNU), Department of
Electronic Systems.

My research focuses on robust statistical estimation,
machine learning, information theory, wireless
communications, and emerging quantum technologies.

My professional background combines academic research
with extensive industrial experience in embedded DSP,
wireless systems, satellite communications, and navigation.

## Research Interests

- Robust estimation and trustworthy machine learning
- RF machine learning and wireless communications
- Quantum information geometry and robust quantum control
- Navigation, sensing, and autonomous systems
- Information theory and joint source-channel coding

## Publications

My research has appeared in IEEE Transactions on
Information Theory, IEEE Transactions on Communications,
IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications,
and IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation.
