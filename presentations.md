## Conference Presentations

### IEEE Quantum Week 2026 — Toronto, Canada
**September 16, 2026**

**Toward Robust Real-Time Bosonic Decoding with Geometry-Aware Neural Networks**

Presented in the QML Technical Papers Track at IEEE Quantum Week 2026 (QCE26). Joint work with Stefan Werner.

Introduced a geometry-aware spectral architecture for low-latency bosonic decoding, including Laplace–Beltrami and Fourier representations, robust finite-shot quantum-state reconstruction, quaternion- and complex-valued neural models, and FPGA-oriented implementation considerations.

## Research Project Presentations

### WASP–Aalto University — SAAB Technologies, Helsinki
**August 23, 2024**

**Repeaters in the Electromagnetic Environment**

Poster presentation for the WASP–Aalto collaborative postdoctoral project *Reliable Array Processing*.

Presented repeater-assisted massive MIMO for 5G/6G systems, including low-cost amplify-and-forward repeaters, Eisenstein-integer deployment geometries, and physical-layer security concepts using staggered repeater delays and correlated artificial multipath to complicate adversarial direction-of-arrival estimation.

### WASP–Aalto University — Project Milestone Meeting
**August 12, 2024**

**Repeaters, RIS and Electromagnetic Environments**

Presented research at Aalto University on repeater-assisted massive MIMO, network-controlled amplify-and-forward repeaters, RIS-assisted electromagnetic environments, reciprocity calibration, synchronization, stability, and channel estimation.

Discussed research directions involving structured repeater deployment, interference management, adversarial geolocation, jamming resistance, and physical-layer security.
