My professional background combines academic research
with nearly three decades of industrial experience in
digital signal processing, wireless communications,
satellite systems, embedded software, and semiconductor
technology.

I have worked across the semiconductor, defense, telecommunications, satellite communications, and offshore industries in the United States, Canada, Sweden, Norway, Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines, developing algorithms and implementations for communications, navigation, sensing, and real-time signal processing systems.

## Academic Appointments

### Norwegian University of Science and Technology
**Researcher | 2024–2026**

Research in robust estimation, federated learning,
geometric communications, and quantum information
in Professor Stefan Werner's research group.

### Linköping University, Sweden

and wireless security.

## Industrial Research and Engineering

### Creative Radio Design LLC

Founded and operated an independent signal-processing
consultancy serving companies developing wireless,
satellite, embedded DSP, and communication systems.

Selected engagements:

**Lynk Global (2019–2020)**

**Shoof Technologies (2018)**  
Developed estimation algorithms for non-line-of-sight industrial positioning.

**Nokia Bell Labs (2017–2018)**  
Lead DSP engineer architecting IEEE 802.11ac

**General Dynamics SATCOM Technologies (2007–2008)**

and DSP implementations for communication
systems test equipment.

**Texas Instruments (2007)**
