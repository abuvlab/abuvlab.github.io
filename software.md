## C3T — Constant Curvature Curve Tube Codes
**Research software for low-latency analog joint source–channel coding.**

C3T investigates geometric encoding and decoding methods
based on constant-curvature curves in higher-dimensional
signal spaces.

The research develops analog error-correction methods
using geometric curve representations, with applications
to low-latency communications.

The repository contains MATLAB and Python implementations.

**Repository:** [ABUV Lab / C3T](https://github.com/abuvlab/c3t)
**Related publication:**

A. M. Buvarp, R. M. Taylor Jr., K. V. Mishra, L. Mili,
and A. I. Zaghloul,  
"Constant Curvature Curve Tube Codes for Low-Latency
Analog Error Correction,"  
*IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*,
vol. 69, no. 12, pp. 7738–7754, 2023.

## RCVFL — Robust Complex-Valued Federated Learning

Research software for robust complex-valued federated learning in wireless communications.

The project investigates robust aggregation of complex-valued model updates, with emphasis on resistance to corrupted or anomalous client contributions.

**Repository:** [ABUV Lab / RCVFL](https://github.com/abuvlab/rcvfl)
**Related publication:**

A. M. Buvarp and S. Werner,
“Robust Complex-Valued Federated Learning for Secure Wireless Communications,”
submitted to *IEEE Internet of Things Journal*, 2026.
