Research proposal development is an important part of my
academic work, encompassing scientific leadership,
consortium development, industry collaboration, and
national and international research funding initiatives.

The following projects represent proposals and research
initiatives rather than awarded research grants.

## European Research Council

### ICEBERG
**Information, Control, Encoding and Robust Inference through Quantum Geometry**

**Role:** Principal proposer and Principal Investigator  
**Programme:** ERC Starting Grant  
**Status:** Proposal development

Developing an independent research programme connecting
physical dynamics, quantum information geometry,
encoding, control, and robust statistical inference.

## Research Council of Norway

### KVANTELOS / SKJOLD
**Quantum-Enhanced Robust Asynchronous Maritime Navigation for GNSS-Denied Operations**

**Role:** Principal proposer  
**Programme:** Research Council of Norway  
**Collaboration:** Marine Technologies AS  
**Status:** Proposal submitted, 2026

Originated SKJOLD, a research concept addressing robust
and physics-aware inference under noise, calibration
drift, model mismatch, and realistic operating
conditions.

Developed industrial contacts through discussions
with DNV and NEO/HySpex. The concept subsequently
evolved into KVANTELOS, a four-year research proposal
combining quantum inertial sensing, classical inertial
navigation systems, and robust Kalman filtering for
asynchronous quantum–classical state estimation.

### CORRECT
**Continuously Operating Geometric Quantum Error Correction for Bosonic Codes**

**Role:** Principal proposer and Project Manager  
**Programme:** FRIPRO, Research Council of Norway  
**Status:** Proposal submitted, 2026

Research into spectral-geometric bosonic quantum error
correction, geometry-aware decoding, robust statistical
decoding under realistic hardware noise, benchmarking,
and low-latency FPGA implementation.

### TORQ
**Robust Geometric Quantum Control for Scalable Quantum Processors**

**Role:** Originator, Project Manager, and Scientific Lead  
**Programme:** Breakthrough Enabling Technologies, Research Council of Norway  
**Status:** Proposal submitted, 2026

Research into robust quantum control, Hamiltonian
identification, geometry-aware calibration, risk-aware
pulse optimization, drift, crosstalk, and leakage
robustness.

### QUARTZ
**Quaternion-Robust Receivers for Polarization-Diverse Quantum Communications**

**Role:** Principal proposer and Scientific Lead  
**Programme:** Breakthrough Enabling Technologies, Research Council of Norway  
**Status:** Proposal submitted, 2026

Research into robust signal processing and learning
for quantum-enabled communications, including
complex-valued and quaternion-valued receiver
architectures, polarization-diverse quantum
communications, robust statistics, and reusable
benchmarking methods.

## International Research Collaboration

### AFRQ-Sense
**Autonomous Federated Rydberg Quantum Sensing for ISAC and Smart Grids**

**Role:** Initiator of NTNU participation and Proposal Contributor  
**Programme:** Canada–G7–Nordic NordForsk Quantum Technologies  
**Status:** Proposal submitted, September 2026

**Participating institutions:**
- Norwegian University of Science and Technology
- Aalto University
- University of Alberta
- University of Waterloo

Initiated concept development on the NTNU side
and contributed to consortium building.

Developed scientific contributions concerning robust
and physics-aware inference for distributed Rydberg
quantum sensing, including calibration mismatch,
sensor drift, non-Gaussian noise, outliers,
federated learning, and distributed calibration
for heterogeneous quantum sensing nodes.

## Trustworthy Autonomous Systems

### KVIST
**Quantum-Informed Trust and Uncertainty for Safe Autonomous Systems**

**Role:** Principal proposer and Principal Investigator  
**Programme:** DNV Foundation Autonomous Systems Call  
**Status:** Proposal submitted, 2026
